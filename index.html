People, privacy & image consent

Photographs of identifiable people are published in the portfolio only when appropriate permission has been obtained. Clients may separately choose whether their photographs can be shared publicly for portfolio or promotional purposes. Photo-session consent and portfolio/publication consent are separate choices. For minors, permission from a parent or legal guardian is required. Do not copy, download, repost, or use portfolio images without permission from the photographer and, where applicable, the people shown.
